## Every Card Matters. Not Every Card Is Equally Easy.

The Pinyin Explorer deck contains **365 essential monosyllabic Chinese words**, carefully selected from a larger pool of **1,290 high-frequency dictionary entries**.

Every one of these words is important—but they aren't equally difficult.

Some cards have just one meaning, while others have ten or even eleven distinct senses that appear in different contexts. On average, each card contains about three meanings, but not all multi-sense words are equally challenging.

Some are easy to grasp because their meanings naturally connect. For example, **bāo** can mean "to wrap," "package," or "include," while **ān** covers ideas such as "calm," "peaceful," and "safe." Others lend themselves to memorable stories, like **dú** ("to read," "alone," "poison") or **è** ("hungry," "evil," "crocodile").

At the other end of the spectrum are highly polysemous words whose meanings span several different domains. Words like **dòng** ("to move," "cave," "to freeze"), **diàn** ("electricity," "shop," "palace hall"), or **qián** ("front," "money," "hidden," "to dive") require a broader understanding of how Chinese words have developed across different contexts.

The challenge wasn't deciding **what** to teach. Every word belongs in the deck.

The challenge was deciding **when** to teach it.

A simple frequency list isn't enough, and neither is arranging the cards from easiest to hardest. Both approaches eventually create stretches where the workload rises much faster than the learner's confidence.

Instead, Pinyin Explorer uses a carefully engineered progression that unfolds over an entire year.

The deck is divided into twelve monthly sets. Each month is only a little more demanding than the one before, allowing your vocabulary, pattern recognition, and confidence to grow together. Within each month, the cards gradually increase in complexity, with a few more challenging "mini-boss" cards along the way and a satisfying "boss" card at the end of each set.

The result is a learning journey that feels much like a well-designed game. New challenges appear just as your skills have caught up, keeping the experience engaging without becoming overwhelming.

By the time you reach the final card—**shì**, with its **11 distinct senses**—you're no longer facing an impossible obstacle. You're simply ready for it.
