*Last updated: September 2026*

Pīnyīn Explorer is built to respect your privacy. No personal data is collect, there are zero tracking scripts, and no ads.

### App Suite (Pīnyīn Explorer, Pīnyīn A-Z Soundboard, Peekaboo Reader)
* **Zero Telemetry or Tracking:** The macOS applications do not collect, transmit, or monitor usage analytics, device identifiers, or user behavior.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email to use the apps.
* **100% Local Storage:** All preferences and flashcard progress are stored purely on your local device. No user data is ever transmitted to external servers.

### Website & Hosting
* **No Cookies or Tracking Scripts:** This website does not use tracking cookies, cross-site analytics, or advertising scripts.
* **Cloudflare Infrastructure:** Hosted via Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes standard, ephemeral server logs (such as IP addresses) strictly to maintain web security, prevent abuse, and deliver site content reliably.
* **Support Contact:** If you email support directly, your email address is used exclusively to respond to your inquiry and is never shared, sold, or added to mailing lists.

### Your Data Rights
Because Pīnyīn Explorer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, there is no stored user data to inspect, export, modify, or delete.
