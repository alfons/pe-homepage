# Phonetic Neighborhoods

Most learners organize vocabulary according to topics or textbook chapters.

The Pīnyīn Explorer Method introduces an additional organizational principle: phonetic neighborhoods.

Rather than thinking primarily about "food vocabulary" or "HSK Level 3", learners repeatedly revisit the same spoken syllables and discover the morphemes that share them.

For example, the syllable **fù** immediately opens a family of related words:

- 富 — rich
- 父 — father
- 负 — bear
- 副 — auxiliary
- 付 — pay

As vocabulary grows, each syllable becomes an increasingly rich mental landscape.

The result is a lexicon organized around spoken language itself.
