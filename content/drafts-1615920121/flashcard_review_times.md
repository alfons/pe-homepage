# Estimated Review Time for the Complete Leitner Deck

## Summary 

Although the total active review time is only about 10–12 hours for a typical learner, the Leitner intervals intentionally spread those hours over approximately one year. Learning one new card per day allows the deck to grow gradually while maintaining a manageable review load. Most learners can expect all cards to reach Box 5 approximately one month after the final card has been introduced, resulting in a total calendar time of around 13 months.

## Overview

The deck contains:

* **365 cards**
* **1,120 individual senses**
* **Average:** 3.07 senses per card

Each sense is tested individually using a four-option multiple-choice interface. However, progression is determined at the **card level**: **all senses on a card must be answered correctly** for the card to advance to the next Leitner box.

A single incorrect answer resets the **entire card** back to **Box 1**, requiring it to climb through the Leitner system again.

---

## Assumptions

The estimates below measure **active review time only** (thinking, answering, and UI transitions).

They **exclude** optional activities such as:

* reading dictionary entries,
* exploring compounds,
* comparing related words,
* or any additional study.

These behaviours vary enormously between learners and cannot be estimated reliably.

---

## Estimated Time per Sense

| Scenario        | Seconds / Sense | Description                                                                            |
| --------------- | --------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Perfect Run** |         **4 s** | Immediate recognition, minimal hesitation, almost no mistakes.                         |
| **Typical**     |       **7.5 s** | Normal recall, reading the four answer options, and making a decision.                 |
| **Difficult**   |        **12 s** | Frequent hesitation, slower recall, and more careful comparison of the answer options. |

These estimates already include the application's feedback animations and transition times.

---

## Effect of the Leitner System

Moving a card from **Box 1** to **Box 5** requires four successful promotions:

```text
Box 1 → Box 2 → Box 3 → Box 4 → Box 5
```

Unlike conventional flashcard systems, a failed review sends the card back to **Box 1**.

For example:

```text
Box 4
   ↓ (incorrect)
Box 1 → Box 2 → Box 3 → Box 4 → Box 5
```

As a result, mistakes increase the total number of reviews non-linearly, especially for cards with many senses.

---

## Estimated Total Active Review Time

Based on the current deck size and the Leitner simulation, the complete journey from **365 new cards** to **all cards in Box 5** is expected to require approximately:

| Scenario             | Estimated Active Review Time |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------: |
| **Perfect Run**      |              **≈ 6–7 hours** |
| **Typical Learner**  |            **≈ 10–12 hours** |
| **Difficult Recall** |            **≈ 15–18 hours** |

These figures represent cumulative interaction time spread over the entire learning process. In practice, the calendar duration is much longer because the Leitner system intentionally spaces reviews over weeks and months.

---

## Conclusion

Although the deck contains only **365 cards**, the learner interacts with **1,120 individual senses**, and the Leitner reset mechanism means difficult cards are reviewed multiple times before reaching mastery.

Consequently, mastering the complete deck represents roughly **6 to 18 hours of active review**, depending on recall speed and error rate, with additional optional study time determined entirely by the learner's curiosity and exploration habits.
